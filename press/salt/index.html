Description

Step into the arena. SALT is a fast-paced first-person laser tag shooter that tests your movement and your aim. Parkour across unique maps, find new weapons mid-match, and outplay bots, real players, or both, in single player or online.

Fight through the Warehouse, the Trainyard and the Haunted Depot, each lit like a real laser tag arena: pools of light over the bases and objectives, with dark lanes and haze in between, so the other team has to come find you.

Four ways to play

Elimination: two teams race to 15 kills, and three hits take down a vest. Domination: capture the control points and hold them. Capture the Flag: defend your flag while you steal theirs. Free For All: first to 10 eliminations wins, and the bots hunt everyone.

Spooky Mode

Jack-o’-lanterns, wall torches, cages and ghostly sheets fill the Haunted Depot. Host with Spooky Mode on and everyone in the match wears a jack-o’-lantern head, bots included.

Features

  • Parkour movement built for high-mobility fights
  • Four guns that each play differently: Pistol, Laser Rifle, Auto Rifle and Sniper
  • Four modes: Elimination, Domination, Capture the Flag and Free For All, plus a Mixed playlist
  • Bots to play with any time, solo or online, and LAN play
  • Your laser in your color: red, blue, yellow, green or purple
  • Three arenas: the Warehouse, the Trainyard and the Haunted Depot
  • Safe to stream and monetize: the soundtrack is safe for Twitch and YouTube, including monetized videos
